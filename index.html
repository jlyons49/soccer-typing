📖 HOW TO PLAY SOCCER TYPING

Soccer Typing is inspired by classic arcade typing games like Slam Dunk Typing. Your keyboard commands the striker on the pitch!

1. The Approach Run

When each shot begins, the referee blows the whistle and your striker approaches the ball. A green progress bar at the top displays your remaining lead-up time.

2. Pick Your Goal Target

Target badges hover over different zones of the goal:

  • Top Left & Top Right Corners (Top Bins / Upper 90): Longer, more challenging words, but score a huge 1.75x bonus and have a ~94% goal chance because the goalkeeper cannot reach them easily!
  • Bottom Left & Bottom Right (Side Netting): 1.45x score bonus with ~88% goal chance.
  • Center (Power Strike): Shorter words, but the keeper stands right in the middle — you must type fast to beat them!

3. Type to Strike

Press the first letter of any target's word to lock onto that shot. Type the rest of the letters before your lead-up timer expires to unleash the kick!
