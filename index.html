📖 HOW TO PLAY SOCCER TYPING

Soccer Typing is inspired by classic arcade typing games like Slam Dunk Typing. Your keyboard commands the player on the pitch across 5 scalable difficulty levels!

🎯 Mode 1: Shooting Practice

  • During each approach run, target words appear on different parts of the goal.
  • Top & Bottom Corners: Longer words, up to 1.75x points & ~94% goal probability!
  • Center Target: Shorter words, but the goalkeeper can reach them more easily.
  • Type the word before the lead-up timer runs out to unleash your shot!

🏃 Mode 2: Dribbling Drill

  • Dribble 100 meters across the field by typing a word for each touch of the ball.
  • No Individual Word Time Limit: Take as long as you need on each word.
  • Mistype Reset: Mistyping a letter forces you to retype the current word from the beginning!
  • Time Trial Score: Your score is the total elapsed time it takes to cross the entire field.

⚡ Mode 3: Touch Practice

  • Type single letters to juggle and tap the ball with alternating feet (left foot, right foot).
  • 60-Second Timer: Score as many touches as possible before the clock expires.
  • Home Key Distance: Difficulty scales from anchor home keys (A, S, D, F, J, K, L) up to difficult reaches (Z, Q, X, P).
  • Sudden Death: On World Class difficulty, a single mistype fails the drill immediately!
